About NEXTZEN

NEXTZEN Classes & Computer Academy is focused on building strong academic foundations and practical digital skills for the next generation.

Our Vision

To empower students with high-quality academic education and practical computer skills that prepare them for a better future, all within a modern and supportive learning environment.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, tech-enabled, and result-oriented education. We bridge the gap between traditional schooling and modern digital requirements by offering academic classes alongside career-oriented computer training.

Why We Started

We saw a growing need for a local academy that not only supports students in their regular school curriculum (KG to 10th) but also equips them with essential digital literacy. Today, computer skills are just as important as reading and writing. NEXTZEN was founded to bring both under one roof with modern facilities like projector-based classrooms and hands-on computer labs.

What We Offer

Academic Classes (KG-10th)

Comprehensive academic support to build strong fundamentals, solve doubts, and prepare for board exams with personal attention.

Computer Academy

Practical digital training ranging from basic computer literacy (BCC, CCC) to advanced diploma courses (ADCA).

Modern Infrastructure

Learning made engaging through projector-supported smart classrooms and dedicated computer/laptop labs for hands-on practice.
